BThe case for early advice

Right advice.
Stronger decisions.
Better outcomes.

By the time a contractor is on site, most of the money is already committed. The drawings are fixed, the contract is signed, and every change costs more than it would have six months earlier.

Build Ready works in the window before that. We appraise sites, interrogate cost plans, stress-test programmes and surface the risks that usually arrive too late to do anything about. We are not here to sell you a scheme — we are here to tell you what it will really take.

hiding?”

Due diligenceTechnical & site investigation review

Thorough analysis and insight. We read everything you have been handed — and work out what you have not been handed — so the surprises surface at desk stage rather than in a variation order.

  • Technical due diligence for purchase or funding

DHow we work

A clear route from “maybe”
to a signed contract.

We map to the RIBA Plan of Work, so you always know which stage you are in, what has been settled, and what is still open.

01 RIBA 0–1

Brief & feasibility

We agree what success looks like, then test whether the site can deliver it. You get a straight answer on viability before the spending starts.

02 RIBA 1–2

Diligence & risk

We interrogate the information, find the gaps, and put a quantified risk register in front of you with the cost and time attached to each item.

03 RIBA 2–3

Cost & programme

Design is tested against budget and date, not the other way round. Where they do not meet, we show you the options rather than the problem.

04 RIBA 3–4

Procure & commit

The right route, the right tender list, the right contract terms — so the contractor you appoint is the one who can actually deliver it.
